What We Do

Union Activities

Character Study

We study outstanding characters in the Bible. A book is studied systematically, and we come together to share our thoughts on it.

Rep Your Jersey

We have an exciting day where we wear our favorite team's jersey to Church. This is to teach us to be proud of who we are and where we come from.

Traditional Sunday

On this day we rock our brilliant African prints to showcase our rich culture. This helps us deeply appreciate our roots and where we come from.

Get Together

We organize regular social get-togethers to foster fellowship and unity among members, building lasting friendships.

Sunday Service

Where we sing powerful praises, read the Bible, and listen to the inspiring, transformative Word of God weekly.

Outreach

We embark on community outreach activities like visiting the needy, practicing evangelism, and checking up on members in SHS & university.
